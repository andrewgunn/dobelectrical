Our story

Serving Sheffield for 35+ years.

Founded in 1990, D O'Brien Electrical Limited started as a local residential electrical contractor in Sheffield. We built our reputation on quality workmanship, honest service and putting our customers first.

Over the years we've grown to serve all of South Yorkshire for residential work and expanded our commercial services nationwide, delivering fit-outs for national retail and hospitality brands.

Despite that growth, we've kept the family-founded values that got us here: integrity, professionalism and a commitment to excellence. With over 1,000 completed projects and 500+ satisfied customers, every job still gets the same personal attention.
